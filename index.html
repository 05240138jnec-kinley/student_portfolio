About Me

Who I am and where I'm headed.

I'm Kinley Tshering, a Computer Systems and Networking student at Jigme Namgyel Engineering College, Bhutan. I'm passionate about exploring how technology connects the world, from designing networks and securing systems to developing applications that solve real-world problems.
